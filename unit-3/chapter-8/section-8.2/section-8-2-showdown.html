SOURCE A1887 · A federal law

Interstate Commerce Act

Congress passed this law to deal with railroad abuses. It covered railroads that carried goods between states.

“All charges made for any service rendered or to be rendered in the transportation of passengers or property as aforesaid, or in connection therewith, or for the receiving, delivering, storage, or handling of such property, shall be reasonable and just; and every unjust and unreasonable charge for such service is prohibited and declared to be unlawful. . . . [S]uch common carrier shall be deemed guilty of unjust discrimination, which is hereby prohibited and declared to be unlawful.”
Interstate Commerce Act, Sections 1 and 2, February 4, 1887 (24 Stat. 379); National Archives, Milestone Documents.
Word help: aforesaid = mentioned above · charges = prices · reasonable = fair · prohibited = banned · unlawful = against the law · common carrier = a company that carries anyone’s goods · discrimination = unfair different treatment

SOURCE B1909 · A memoir

Random Reminiscences

John D. Rockefeller wrote about his career long after he built Standard Oil. Here he defends big combinations.

“The day of individual competition in large affairs is past and gone—you might just as well argue that we should go back to hand labour and throw away our efficient machines—and the sober good sense of the people will accept this fact when they have studied and tried it out. Just see how the list of stockholders in the great corporations is increasing by leaps and bounds. This means that all these people are becoming partners in great businesses.”
John D. Rockefeller, Random Reminiscences of Men and Events (New York: Doubleday, Page, 1909); text from the National Humanities Center (“Standard Oil”), checked against Hackernoon’s reprint of the Gutenberg text. “Hand labour” is the original British spelling.
Word help: individual competition = many small businesses fighting each other · large affairs = big industries · efficient = does the job with little waste · sober = sensible · stockholders = people who own shares of a company · partners = co-owners
