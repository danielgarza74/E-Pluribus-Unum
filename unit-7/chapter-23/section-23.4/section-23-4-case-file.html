2000Senate testimony

Hilary Rosen, head of the Recording Industry Association of America

Rosen spoke for the big record companies. She explained why they sued Napster.

“. . . in the case of Napster, they never sought licenses before they started this service. . . . it is important to know that these are business negotiations and that the artists and record companies didn’t have a choice at the outset of the businesses to grant licenses because they weren’t sought.”
Hilary Rosen, testimony at the Senate Judiciary Committee hearing “Music on the Internet: Is There an Upside to Downloading?” July 11, 2000, S. Hrg. 106-1060; U.S. Government Publishing Office (govinfo.gov). Sentences between the two parts were left out and marked with an ellipsis; the first word was made lowercase.
Word help: licenses = official permission to use something · litigation = a court case · negotiations = talks to make a deal · outset = the start

2011A presidential statement

President Barack Obama

The Egyptian president had just stepped down after weeks of huge protests. The American President spoke about it.

“The people of Egypt have spoken, their voices have been heard, and Egypt will never be the same. By stepping down, President Mubarak responded to the Egyptian people’s hunger for change. But this is not the end of Egypt’s transition, it’s a beginning. . . . For Egyptians have made it clear that nothing less than genuine democracy will carry the day.”
Barack Obama, remarks on the situation in Egypt, February 11, 2011; Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents, U.S. Government Publishing Office (govinfo.gov). A few sentences in the middle were left out and marked with an ellipsis.

2012A letter to investors

Mark Zuckerberg, founder of Facebook

Zuckerberg wrote this letter when Facebook asked the public to buy its stock. He explained what Facebook is for.

“Facebook was not originally created to be a company. It was built to accomplish a social mission — to make the world more open and connected. . . . There is a huge need and a huge opportunity to get everyone in the world connected, to give everyone a voice and to help transform society for the future.”
Mark Zuckerberg, “Letter from Mark Zuckerberg,” in Facebook, Inc., Form S-1 registration statement, U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, February 1, 2012. The em dash is in the original. Some sentences were left out and marked with an ellipsis.
Word help: mission = a big goal · connected = joined together · opportunity = a chance · transform = change in a big way

2018An opening statement

Mark Zuckerberg, speaking to the U.S. Senate

Six years later, Zuckerberg testified to the Senate. Lawmakers asked about fake news, election meddling, and private data.

“But it’s clear now that we didn’t do enough to prevent these tools from being used for harm as well. And that goes for fake news, for foreign interference in elections, and hate speech, as well as developers and data privacy. We didn’t take a broad enough view of our responsibility, and that was a big mistake. And it was my mistake. And I’m sorry. I started Facebook, I run it, and I’m responsible for what happens here.”
Mark Zuckerberg, opening statement to the Senate Judiciary and Commerce Committees, April 10, 2018; American Rhetoric (transcript). Quoted as in the transcript.
Word help: interference = getting in the way · responsibility = a duty · testimony = what a witness says in a hearing · foreign = from another country
